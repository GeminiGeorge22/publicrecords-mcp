## First comment (post immediately after the story is live)

I maintain Etsy Search Scraper on Apify Store (publicrecords/etsy-search-scraper). It turns an Etsy keyword, market page, or category URL into listing rows: price, reviews, Bestseller and Star Seller badges, ad flags, shop, and rank.

You run it on your own Apify account (free tier is enough to try the example `{"queries": ["ceramic mug"], "maxPages": 1}`), then pay per listing row. Not affiliated with Etsy.

Companion shop-sales tracker: https://apify.com/publicrecords/etsy-shop-velocity
How-to: https://geminigeorge22.github.io/etsy-search-scraper/
MCP pin: https://mcp.apify.com?tools=publicrecords/etsy-search-scraper
n8n / Make recipes: https://github.com/GeminiGeorge22/publicrecords-mcp/tree/main/templates

Happy to answer questions about inputs, pricing, or what it does not do (it cannot read Etsy Shop Manager).
