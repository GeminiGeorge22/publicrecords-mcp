## Title

Scrape Etsy search results into a spreadsheet in 5 minutes (Public Records)

## Outline

1. The question: what does Etsy actually show for this keyword?
2. Open the Store page https://apify.com/publicrecords/etsy-search-scraper and run the example `{"queries": ["ceramic mug"], "maxPages": 1}` on your own Apify account (free tier is enough to try it).
3. Download the dataset and glance at price, badges, ads, and rank.
4. Optional: pin MCP at https://mcp.apify.com?tools=publicrecords/etsy-search-scraper or import the n8n/Make recipe from https://github.com/GeminiGeorge22/publicrecords-mcp/tree/main/templates
5. Join shop names to Etsy Shop Sales Tracker if you care about velocity: https://apify.com/publicrecords/etsy-shop-velocity
