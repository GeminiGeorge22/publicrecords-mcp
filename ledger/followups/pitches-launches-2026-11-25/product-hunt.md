## Description

Etsy sellers and researchers often need a table of what Etsy actually shows for a keyword — price, reviews, Bestseller and Star Seller badges, ad flags, shop, and rank — not a screenshot.

Etsy Search Scraper, by Public Records on Apify, turns a keyword, market page, or category URL into one row per listing. You run it on your own Apify account (free tier available) and pay per listing row.

Try the Store example, or follow the short how-to. Companion Actor: Etsy Shop Sales Tracker, for daily shop counters. Not affiliated with Etsy, Inc.

Store: https://apify.com/publicrecords/etsy-search-scraper
How-to: https://geminigeorge22.github.io/etsy-search-scraper/
